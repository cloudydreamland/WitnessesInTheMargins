Climate policy briefing (excerpt).

Global mean surface temperature has risen by approximately 1.1 degrees Celsius since pre-industrial times [1]. Arctic sea ice extent has declined by more than ten per cent per decade since satellite records began [2]. Recent modelling suggests polar amplification may add a further 0.3 degrees by 2100 [3].

Policy. By pricing carbon, economies can internalise emission costs and redirect investment toward renewable energy [4].

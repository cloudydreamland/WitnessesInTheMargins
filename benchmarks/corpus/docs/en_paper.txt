Abstract. Scaling laws suggest compute-optimal training regimes. Large language models exhibit emergent capabilities when scaled beyond a critical compute threshold [1]. Retrieval augmentation has become standard in production systems. Retrieval-augmented generation grounds model outputs in external evidence and reduces hallucination [2].

We further examine reasoning. Chain-of-thought prompting improves arithmetic reasoning by explicitly eliciting intermediate steps [3]. Some authors even claim that RLHF fully solves the alignment problem once and for all [4].
